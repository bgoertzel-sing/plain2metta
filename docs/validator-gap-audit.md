## Appendix N/P categories not yet covered

The design summary for Appendices N/P recommends validation beyond v0.1's structural profile. These categories are not yet implemented:

- Information-flow checks, including whether data dependencies, inputs, outputs, and leakage paths are valid.
- Time and temporal-availability checks, including ordering, freshness, availability windows, and time-series-specific constraints.
- Broader methodology validation beyond the first ML/time-series slices, including experiment design, leakage patterns beyond explicit preprocessing-order, future/label-as-feature, and random/shuffled split wording, metric appropriateness beyond the current conservative classification-vs-forecast keyword review, and richer baseline/uncertainty semantics beyond generic-vs-named comparator/method evidence.
- Process/resource discipline, including runtime resources, scheduling, deployment/process constraints, and backend artifact lifecycle.
- Security/privacy obligations, including secrets handling, PII/privacy constraints, access boundaries, and unsafe operational actions.
- Phase 2 semantic objects (`Scope`, `EpistemicStatus`, `Evidence`, `Interpretation`, `Bridge`) and associated validation.
- Phase 3 facets for witnesses/backend artifacts, process/resource placeholders, and revisions.
- SUMO/EXPO/Hyperseed bridge-table validation.
- PLN confidence propagation or contradiction handling.
- Rholang and MeTTa-IL target-profile validation.

## Highest-priority v0.2 gaps

1. **Deepen methodology validation for the ML/time-series fixture.** The first v0.2 slices now cover metric declaration, a conservative metric/task appropriateness review, horizon/frequency, reproducibility evidence, train-only preprocessing fit scope, explicit preprocessing-order review, future/label-as-feature leakage review, temporal split-order review, baseline comparison, named comparator review, uncertainty/error bars, and named uncertainty-method review. Next extend toward richer comparator/uncertainty semantics and stronger metric appropriateness once target/task facets are explicit.
2. **Information-flow and temporal availability checks.** These are central to Plain-to-SpecAtom validation and can remain conservative: detect declared inputs/outputs, missing freshness/availability assumptions, and temporally impossible coverage claims without inferring deep semantics.
3. **Security/privacy obligation scaffolding.** Add a small profile for secrets/PII/access-control claims that defaults to Unknown/question when operational or privacy-sensitive requirements lack evidence. This directly supports the profile's no-hallucinated-executable-semantics rule.

Process/resource discipline should follow once Phase 3 placeholders or backend artifact records exist; until then, it has less schema surface to validate.
